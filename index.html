      To practice a section: drag the brackets on the strip of bars, or tap a note (on the strip or in the drop notes) and press Set start, then do the same for the end. Dragging across the strip sets a new section in one go, and dragging the drop notes up or down moves through the piece. Play runs it with Repeat or Once; Step and Back move one note or chord at a time and wrap around inside it. Lit keys show finger numbers, 1 = thumb to 5 = little finger. When a piece comes in without fingering (or with only some), Pianissimo suggests the rest and writes it into the score, marked as a suggestion; Edit fingering (under More controls) changes any finger, and your changes go into the score when you press Save fingering or close the piece. A printed finger you change stays in the score as the alternative. MIDI files have no place for fingering, so for them it's kept in the player's own copy, and when a MIDI file keeps both hands on one track the hands are split at middle C. With a MIDI keyboard connected, Wait for me lights the next note for the hand you choose and moves on when you play it; the other hand is played for you, in time.

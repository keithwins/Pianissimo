# Practice lists, hard moves, and richer sections

November 1, 2026. Keith and Claude. Built (in the page) tonight.

## Saved sections now keep more

A saved section (Section menu, "Save section") also keeps:

- **Status**: Working on it, Mastered, or Parked. The Saved sections list marks them ▸ ✓ ·.
- **Hand** it is for: Both, Right, Left.
- **A running tally**, kept up automatically from Wait for me and playing: passes, clean passes (no extra notes), the tempo of the best clean pass, the last day, and seconds played.
- **How it was last drilled** (hard-moves settings), so the drill opens the way you left it.

All of this lives in the piece's sidecar (the practice file), beside the section's start and end.

## Drill the hard moves

Section menu, "Drill the hard moves". Looks at one hand's notes in the selected passage and finds where the hand has to leave its position:

- a jump of a sixth or more, or
- a step wider than the two fingers used can cover with the hand still (the same finger twice, 2 then 5, and so on), or
- a wide cross-over or cross-under.

For each move it makes short fragments (2 to 5 notes, you choose; 3 and 4 by default) that hold both notes of the move, with the move at different places in the fragment. Each fragment is written out as its own short bar, repeated as many times as you choose, in even eighth notes at about half the usual tempo (it trains where the hand goes, not the rhythm). Fingering is carried over from the piece. For a chord, the left hand uses its lowest note and the right hand its highest.

It is built the way exercises are (a small score), so the staff, falling notes, Wait for me, the section chips and tempo all work on it. "← Piece" in the title bar goes back to the same section.

Limits: the reach table is a rough model of an average hand; a passage with no fingering is judged by interval size alone; at most 48 fragments per drill.

## Practice lists

"Lists" in the top bar (and Settings, "Practice lists"). A list holds three kinds of item: a **section** of a piece, the **hard-moves drill** for a section, or an **exercise**. Items are references, not copies. "Add to a list" is in the Section menu, in the hard-moves card, and in the list itself ("Add what's open now").

A list is a pool, not a queue:

- **Pick for me** chooses what needs work most: not parked, not mastered (those count for much less), not practised lately, few clean passes, with a little chance so it varies.
- **In order** and **Shuffled** also work. While a list is running, a **Next ›** button sits in the top bar.

Lists are kept in this browser and in the music folder's players file. Not built: lists inside lists (an item type can be added later), and exercise items that remember a practice tally.

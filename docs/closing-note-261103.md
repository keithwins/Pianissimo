# Exercises: the closing note, and steady 4/4

November 3, 2026. Keith and Claude.

- Exercises are always written in 4/4. A last bar that isn't full is filled out with rests, so there are no 4/8, 5/8 or 7/8 bars.
- A figure that ends on the note it began on (up and down a scale, an arpeggio, a five-finger pattern that comes home) has a **closing note**. The exercise opens with its **cycle** (everything before that note) as the section, shown as "The cycle" on the section button. Looping goes round the cycle, so the tonic isn't played twice.
- **Once** (Loop off) plays the cycle and then the closing note, in Play and in Wait for me. "Clear section" or selecting the whole piece includes it too.
- Up-only runs, and patterns that end elsewhere than they began, have no closing note and open on the whole exercise as before.
- The loop point can fall mid-bar; sections can start and end on any note.
- Clef changes in a hand's staff no longer happen at middle C itself (it stays in the clef already being read).
